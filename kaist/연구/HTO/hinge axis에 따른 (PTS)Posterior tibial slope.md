**The Ideal Hinge Axis Position to Reduce Tibial Slope in Opening-Wedge High Tibial Osteotomy Includes Proximalization-Extension and Internal Rotation**
Claire D. Eliasberg, M.D., Kyle J. Hancock, M.D., Erica Swartwout, B.A., Hugo Robichaud, P.Eng., and Anil S. Ranawat, M.D

# Intro
HTO는 주로 내측 관절 염 (unicompartmental medial osteoarthritis)이나 varus tibiofemoral alignment 상황에서 주로 사용되는 수술
HTO의 inidication이 확장함에, 따라 ACL 결함에 대한 경골 절골술 관심이 커진다. TIBIA SLOPE의 증가는 ACL tesars, ACL deficiency, ACL 재건 실패등을 야기

HTO는 weight bearing 회복, precise manipulation of correction intraoperatively
의 장점이 있지만, tibia slope변화 라는 단점도 있음

The hinge axis position has been identified as a significant determinant in altering the PTS during HTO [26,31,35]
Therefore, during preparation for medial opening wedge HTO, careful preoperative planning is essential not only to determine the degree of correction in the coronal plane but also to properly assess the change in the PTS that will arise as a result of this coronal-plane correction, a variable that can be challenging to predict.

본 연구의 목적은 HTO 수술에서 hinge axis의 위치가 PTS에 어떤 영향을 미치는지 알아내는 것
internal vs external / proximalization extnesion vs distalizaiton-flexion
